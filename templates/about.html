About me

From pewter window shopping to printing my own adventures

The early years

Late-'80s D&D kid

I started playing Dungeons & Dragons in elementary school in the late '80s. From there I dove into all kinds of RPGs: Rifts, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles & Other Strangeness and plenty more. I loved the pewter minis in the game shop windows, but I never had any of my own.

Rediscovering the magic

Back at the table

Years later, on a two-family vacation with great friends, we played a session of D&D with the then-seven-year-olds. It all came rushing back. When I turned 40, I decided I wanted to see my friends more often, so we started a weekly game that's been going strong across three campaigns and twice as many years. I also ran a campaign for my son and his friends for about five years.

The first paint job

Everyone starts somewhere

For my first campaign I had someone print a mini through an online service. That was also my first paint job ever. Then I discovered HeroForge and used it to make my player characters for the second campaign.

About three years ago, my partner got me my first printer, and I printed my first minis. That was so much fun that I started printing everything I needed for the game I was running.

Homework & hobbies

Painting through homework

I needed something to do while keeping my son company during homework, something that wouldn't distract him. So I picked up some spray paint and a few paints and got started. Middle school came with a lot of homework, so I got a lot of practice. The kids spent a good while in the Wild Beyond the Witchlight, which is why you'll see so much carnival stuff and so many rabbit folk in my work.

Comic book dreams

The statues I never bought

I've loved comics since I was a kid and always admired the statues in shop windows. Just like the pewter minis, I never bought any. Now that I can print them, I get to make the statues I always dreamed of owning.

See the statues

Maybe you'll be inspired too

I'd never painted a mini until a few years ago, and now it's my favorite way to relax. I hope you like what you see here, and maybe feel like picking up a brush yourself.
