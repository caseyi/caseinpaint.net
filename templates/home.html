How it's made

From file to finished piece

  1. Print. Resin for fine detail, FDM for big, tough pieces. Supports placed by hand so they vanish later.
  2. Prep & prime. Wash, cure, clean up, then an airbrushed zenithal prime that maps out the light.
  3. Paint. Base colors, washes, layers and highlights, then the fun stuff: glow, blood, rust and basing.
