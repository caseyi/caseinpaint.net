Quick answers

How long does it take?

Smaller pieces take a few days. Large projects take up to a week. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

What about shipping?

Shipping costs depend on where you are. Everything is packed carefully in foam to survive the trip.

Can I pick it up?

Yes. If you're in the Seattle area, local pickup is available and saves on shipping. Just mention it when you get in touch.

How does payment work?

50% deposit to start, 50% before shipping or pickup. PayPal and Venmo both work.

What's included?

Every model comes on a base with a velvet bottom. Painted pieces get a protective varnish, and I'll send photos of the finished piece before it ships.

Can I send my own file?

Absolutely: HeroForge files, files from licensed STL stores, or your own designs.
